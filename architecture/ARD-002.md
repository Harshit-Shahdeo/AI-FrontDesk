Status: Accepted

Date: 2026-07-30

Context

The initial target market is Indian small and medium businesses.

The first version of the platform will be manually onboarded, with approximately the first 10–20 businesses configured directly by the development team.

The platform is designed to be WhatsApp-first, where both business owners and customers interact with the system through WhatsApp rather than a traditional web dashboard.

Requesting OTP verification during onboarding introduces additional friction and may reduce trust, particularly in the target market where users are often cautious about sharing OTPs due to common scam awareness.

Decision

For Version 1:

Businesses will be onboarded manually.
The owner's phone number will be recorded during onboarding.
No OTP verification will be required.
Incoming WhatsApp messages will determine identity using the sender's phone number.

Identity resolution:

Incoming WhatsApp Message
        │
        ▼
Sender Phone Number
        │
        ▼
Matches Registered Owner Phone?
      /         \
    Yes         No
     │           │
Owner Flow   Customer Flow

Administrative actions are available only through the Owner Flow.

Customer interactions use the Customer Flow.

Future Evolution

As the platform moves toward self-service onboarding:

Phone ownership verification (OTP or another verification mechanism) may be introduced.
A web dashboard may be added for advanced configuration.
JWT or another HTTP authentication mechanism may be introduced for dashboard/API access.

These future additions must not affect the WhatsApp-based identity resolution used for incoming messages.

Consequences
Advantages
Extremely simple onboarding
Matches the WhatsApp-first product philosophy
No login or password required
Lower friction for initial customers
Faster deployment for early adopters
Trade-offs
Manual onboarding effort
Phone number changes require manual support
Not suitable for fully automated self-service onboarding
